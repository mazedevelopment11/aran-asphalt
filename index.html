Who we are

A strategic company of Halabja Group

Aran Asphalt has specialised in asphalt production since 2007. Alongside Halabja Concrete, it is a principal supplier to Halabja General Contracting.

The plant sits 35 km south-east of Sulaymaniyah, near Warmawa. Besides the mixing plants, laboratories and mixers, the site holds offices, warehouses, parking and planted gardens. What sets it apart is a heating system that runs on LPG gas.

Quality infrastructure solutions

From the plant to the pavement

Core

Asphalt production

Hot-mix asphalt for wearing, binder and stabiliser courses, produced to specification and supplied to contractors across the region.

  • LPG-heated drying and mixing
  • Batch checks on temperature and composition
  • Lab-verified before dispatch
Service

Paving & resurfacing

New asphalt surfaces for new builds, or an overlay that brings worn roads, driveways and parking areas back into service.

  • Roads, estates and parking
  • Single and multi-course paving
  • Crushed stone base preparation
Service

Road maintenance & repair

Regular maintenance extends a road's working life. We fill potholes, seal cracks, fix drainage and restore safe surfaces.

  • Removal of failed layers
  • Pothole and crack repair
  • Drainage correction

Sustainability in asphalt

Cleaner heat, less waste
